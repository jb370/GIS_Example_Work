Software: QGIS 3.44.15

1. OVERVIEW
Enriched the filtered Roadway Subblock layer with permit and service
request counts and dates, flagged subblocks with both, and created a
version of the permit data snapped to the roadway.

2. DATA SOURCES
- Construction Permits (DDOT_TOPS MapServer, layer 23)
- Roadway Subblock (Transportation_WebMercator MapServer, layer 162)
- Cityworks Service Requests (DDOT/Cityworks MapServer, layer 1)
All three are loaded as REST layers. Filtered local copies are stored
in [geopackage name].gpkg. A GeoPackage was used in place of an Esri
file geodatabase because the work was done in QGIS.

3. FILTERS APPLIED
Permits:
  ("IsExcavation" = 1 OR "IsPaving" = 1)
  AND "ExpirationDate" >= 2025-10-01
  AND "Renewal" = 2
  AND "StatusDescription" = 'Issued'
  Interpretation: [e.g., Renewal = 2 means renewal permit; confirmed
  from unique values]

Roadway Subblock: 
  "Road Type" = (1, 2, 3), for keeping streets, service lanes, and ramps.
  Note: the field uses coded values; stored codes were 1, 2, 3.

Cityworks Requests:
  STATUS NOT IN ('CLOSED','CANCEL','COMPLETE')
  AND DESCRIPTION IN ('ROADWAY REPAIR','POTHOLES','STREET REPAVING',
  'STREET REPAIR','STREET PAVING SCHEDU')
  AND INITIATEDDATE >= 2025-10-01

4. PROCESSING STEPS
a. Reprojected all layers to [CRS, e.g. EPSG:3857].
b. Snapped permits to the roadway with "Snap geometries to layer":
   tolerance 10 meters, behavior prefer closest point, insert extra
   vertices. Output: permits_snapped.
c. Joined each permit to its nearest subblock (Join attributes by
   nearest), then aggregated by subblock ID to get permit_count and
   permit_max_exp.
d. Repeated for service requests with a maximum distance of 40 meters,
   producing sr_count and sr_max_initiate.
e. Joined the results back to all filtered subblocks (no records
   discarded) and replaced NULL counts with 0.
f. Created has_both = 1 where permit_count > 0 AND sr_count > 0.

5. RESULTS
- Filtered subblocks: 2479 (SPR_CWR_ENRIRCHED in folder)
- Subblocks with permits: 2479 (Permit_count in the Feature Table)
- Subblocks with service requests: 2479 (REQUEST_count in the Feature Table)
- Subblocks with both: 103
- Sum of permit_count: 7934 
- Sum of sr_count: 134 

6. ASSUMPTIONS AND LIMITATIONS
- Each permit and request is assigned to one subblock (the nearest),
  so there is no double counting at intersections.
- A permit near parallel streets could snap to the wrong block;
  I spot-checked the largest moves.
- Dates were compared against 2025-10-01 as stored; time zone
  differences (UTC) were not adjusted.
- Service requests were not snapped, as only permits were required.
- [Anything else: odd values, NULLs, data quality issues.]
